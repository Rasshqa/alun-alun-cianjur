# DESIGN.md — Portal Pintar Alun-Alun Cianjur

## Identity
Portal wisata interaktif untuk Alun-Alun Cianjur. Audience: wisatawan, warga lokal, pelajar di HP mid-range. Bukan app korporat, bukan landing page startup.

## Personality
Hangat, informatif, membumi. Seperti papan informasi kayu yang dipasang di taman, bukan billboard digital. Bahasa santai tapi sopan.

## Palette
- **Primary (Earth/Coklat):** #3E2723 (900), #5D4037 (700), #795548 (500), #A1887F (300), #D7CCC8 (100), #EFEBE9 (50)
- **Accent (Islam/Hijau):** #1B5E20 (700), #2E7D32 (600), #388E3C (500), #4CAF50 (400)
- **Highlight (Emas):** #C8A951 (500) — sparingly, on gates/heritage elements only
- **Base:** white (#FFFFFF) cards on #EFEBE9 background
- Reason: coklat = tanah/kayu Sunda, hijau = identitas Islam Kota Santri, emas = gerbang kuning keemasan Alun-Alun

## Typography
- System font stack: -apple-system, system-ui, sans-serif
- Reason: zero download cost, renders natively on every phone, no brand font needed for a utility app
- Headings: semibold, earth-800
- Body: regular, earth-700
- Small/meta: earth-400

## Dial
ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

## Motif
Rounded cards with subtle earth-100 borders. No decorative stripes, no gradients, no glow. Card elevation via a single light shadow on the focused/active card only. The green accent appears only on: status badges (buka/tutup), the active bottom nav item, and the Islam-pilar heritage tags.
